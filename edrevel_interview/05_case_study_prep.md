## Practice Case 1: Pharmaceutical Company — Compliance Training

**Scenario:**
> "PharmaCorp has 3,000 employees across India and the US. They need all employees to complete mandatory GxP compliance training every year. Currently: 60% completion rate, tracked in Excel by 2 HR admins, certificates issued manually via email. Audit is in 3 months."

**Your discovery questions:**
- How many training programs are mandatory? Different for different roles?
- What's the consequence of an employee failing compliance? (audit fine, job risk?)
- What devices do employees use? Do field staff have smartphones?
- Is there an existing HR system we need to integrate with? (SAP, Workday)
- Who manages content today? Do they want to keep updating it themselves?

**Your solution design:**
| Pain Point | Edrevel Feature |
|-----------|----------------|
| Excel tracking | Automated completion dashboard — real-time, by department |
| Manual certificates | Auto-issue digital certificates on completion |
| Low completion (60%) | Role-based assignment + automated reminder sequences |
| Different roles need different content | AI-powered learning paths by job role |
| Audit in 3 months | Export-ready compliance reports in 1 click |
| No mobile access for field staff | Mobile-first content + offline access |

**Your proposed phases:**
- **Week 1-2:** Platform setup, import existing users from HR system, migrate top 10 mandatory courses
- **Week 3:** Admin training, configure automated reminders (Day 1, 7, 14 before deadline)
- **Week 4:** Go-live with communication campaign to employees
- **Month 2:** Mid-point review — monitor completion rates, address blockers
- **Month 3:** Pre-audit compliance report ready

**Expected ROI:**
- HR admin time saved: 2 people × 10 hours/week on manual tracking = 80 hours/month saved
- Completion rate target: 60% → 90% in 90 days
- Audit confidence: real-time dashboard instead of scrambling through Excel

## Practice Case 2: Tech Company — Employee Upskilling

**Scenario:**
> "TechStartup has 800 engineers and PMs. Leadership wants to build a "culture of learning" — but currently L&D has no budget and no dedicated team. 70% of employees say they don't know what skills to develop. The CEO wants to see a skills report for the board in 6 months."

**Discovery questions:**
- What skills are most critical for company growth in the next 12 months? (AI/ML? Cloud? Product management?)
- Do you have a competency framework? Or do we build from scratch?
- What's your current learning budget? What's the cost of NOT developing skills? (attrition, hiring externally)
- How do you measure performance today? (performance reviews — can we connect learning to this?)

**Solution design:**
| Need | Edrevel Feature |
|------|----------------|
| "Don't know what to learn" | SkillsPro engine — personalized recommendations by role |
| No competency framework | Build skills taxonomy using job roles → AI suggests mapping |
| CEO wants skills report | Skills coverage dashboard — company-wide heat map |
| No L&D team | AI Course Creator — their engineers can generate courses themselves |
| Culture of learning | Social learning features, learning leaderboards, peer recommendations |

**Storyline for CEO:**
> "In 6 months, you'll have:
> 1. A live skills map showing every role's current vs. required competencies
> 2. Each of your 800 employees with a personalized learning path
> 3. A board-ready report: 'We closed X skill gaps, Y% of employees actively developing Z skills'
> 4. And your L&D 'team' is now your employees + AI — no additional headcount"

## Practice Case 3: Healthcare Network — Onboarding

**Scenario:**
> "HealthNetwork onboards 200 new nurses and doctors every quarter. Current onboarding: 2-week classroom training, 50% forget key protocols by month 2, preceptors (mentors) spend 40% of their time on basic orientation questions."

**Solution:**
- Replace 2-week classroom → blended: 3-day in-person (critical skills) + self-paced Edrevel for knowledge content
- Microlearning: key protocols available as 3-minute "how-to" videos searchable on mobile
- AI Course Creator: generate protocol updates as courses in hours, not weeks
- Preceptor dashboard: see where each new hire is struggling before 1-on-1 meetings
- Knowledge checks at Day 30, 60, 90 to reinforce retention

**Metric to promise:** Preceptor time on basic questions from 40% → under 15%
