# Edrevel AI — Company & Role Analysis

## What is Edrevel?
- **AI-powered Learning Experience Platform (LXP)**
- Revolutionizing **education, workforce development, and compliance training**
- Target customers: companies doing L&D (Learning & Development) and compliance training

## Edrevel's Core Products (know these!)
| Product | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI Course Creator** | Automatically generates course content using AI — saves L&D teams weeks of manual work |
| **SkillsPro Engine** | Maps employee skills, identifies gaps, recommends personalized learning paths |
| **Learning Insights Dashboards** | Analytics on learner engagement, completion rates, ROI |

## LXP vs LMS — Critical Difference
| LMS (Learning Management System) | LXP (Learning Experience Platform) |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Admin-driven, push learning | Learner-driven, pull learning |
| Structured courses | Personalized pathways |
| Compliance-focused | Engagement + skill-building |
| e.g., Moodle, Cornerstone | e.g., Edrevel, Degreed, EdCast |
| Top-down: "you must complete this" | Bottom-up: "here's what you might love" |

## The Role: Technical / Business Analyst
**You are the bridge between:**
- Clients ↔ Technology
- Business goals ↔ Platform capabilities
- Complex AI features ↔ Simple outcomes clients understand

**3 hats you'll wear:**
1. **Analyst** — gather requirements, understand client pain points
2. **Consultant** — map client needs to Edrevel's modules, design solutions
3. **Storyteller** — write proposals, present demos, articulate ROI

## Why This Role is Strategic
- Edrevel is growing — you'll shape how clients adopt AI in learning
- You'll see patterns across many clients → feed product roadmap
- Part sales engineer + part project manager + part data analyst

## Key Industries You'll Serve
- **Corporate L&D**: onboarding, upskilling, leadership development
- **Compliance training**: HIPAA, GDPR, safety certifications — mandatory completions
- **Workforce development**: reskilling at scale (e.g., a bank training 10,000 employees on AI)

## What "Digital Transformation of L&D" Means
Old way: HR creates PDF courses → employees sit through them → forget 90% in a week
Edrevel way: AI personalizes content → bite-sized → skill-tracked → data proves ROI
