# Smart Questions to Ask the Interviewer

## About the Role
1. "What does success look like in the first 90 days for this role?"
2. "What are the most common challenges new BAs face when onboarding clients on Edrevel?"
3. "How much of the role is client-facing vs. internal (product/engineering collaboration)?"
4. "What does the handoff look like between sales and the BA once a client signs?"

## About the Product & Team
5. "What Edrevel features are clients asking for most right now? Where are the biggest gaps?"
6. "How does the BA team feed insights back into the product roadmap — is there a formal process?"
7. "What's the tech stack the BA works with day-to-day — any internal tools, CRM, analytics platforms?"

## About Growth & Company
8. "Edrevel is described as 'rapidly expanding' — what markets or verticals are you prioritizing in the next 12 months?"
9. "Where do strong BAs typically grow into — product management, solutions architecture, customer success leadership?"
10. "What's the biggest opportunity you see in the AI-in-learning space that Edrevel is positioned to capture?"

## Show You've Done Your Homework
11. "I noticed Edrevel focuses on both compliance and skills development — do clients typically come in for one use case and expand to the other, or are they usually bought together?"
12. "How do clients typically compare Edrevel to competitors like Degreed or EdCast during the evaluation process? What's the key differentiator that wins deals?"

---

## One Power Closing Statement
> "Everything I've heard today confirms this is exactly the kind of role I'm looking for — where I can bring analytical rigour, client empathy, and a genuine passion for how AI is reshaping learning. I'm very interested. What are the next steps in the process?"

---

## Red Flags to Listen For
- "The BA is also expected to handle implementation" → scope creep, watch out
- "Clients are very enterprise, 12-month sales cycle" → may feel slow, check pace
- "We're still figuring out the process" → early-stage chaos, decide if you're OK with ambiguity

## Green Flags
- Clear division between pre-sales and post-sales BA work
- Product team actively listening to client feedback
- Defined onboarding ramp (first 30-60-90 day plan exists)
- Genuine excitement about AI roadmap
