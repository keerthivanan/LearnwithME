# One-Page Cheat Sheet — Read This Before the Interview

## Edrevel in 3 Sentences
Edrevel is an AI-powered Learning Experience Platform (LXP) that helps organizations train, upskill, and certify their workforce. It combines AI Course Creator (builds courses in hours), SkillsPro Engine (personalizes learning paths), and Insights Dashboards (proves training ROI). Unlike a traditional LMS that just tracks completions, Edrevel makes learning intelligent and personalized.

- Translate business needs into platform configurations
- Deliver proposals, workshops, and measurable outcomes

## The 5 Discovery Questions (Memorize These)
1. "How does training work today — walk me through the process end-to-end?"
2. "What's the biggest pain point you're trying to solve?"
3. "How do you currently measure success of training?"
4. "Who are the stakeholders who need to see value — learners, managers, compliance officers, C-suite?"
5. "What does success look like for you in 12 months?"

Instead of "our platform is good" → say:
- "Industry benchmark for compliance completion is 65% — our clients average 91%"
- "Typical L&D team spends 80% of time building content — AI Course Creator reduces that to 20%"
- "One BA helped a 2,000-person retailer reduce onboarding time from 3 weeks to 9 days"

---

## Jargon to Use Confidently
- **LXP / LMS** — know the difference cold
- **Compliance training** — mandatory regulatory training (HIPAA, GDPR, SOX, safety)
- **L&D** — Learning & Development team inside a company
- **Learner engagement** — are employees actually using the platform?
- **Skills taxonomy** — structured map of all skills in an organization
- **xAPI** — modern standard for tracking any learning activity
- **Kirkpatrick Model** — 4-level framework to evaluate training effectiveness
- **MoSCoW** — prioritization framework (Must/Should/Could/Won't)
- **User Story** — "As a [user], I want [feature] so that [outcome]"

## Situational Responses (30-second versions)

**"Tell me about a time you failed"**
> "I once built a full solution brief before truly validating the client's real priority. They wanted simplicity, I gave them a 20-page doc. Learned: always confirm 'what's the one thing that matters most' before you design anything."

**"Why Edrevel?"**
> "AI is genuinely changing what's possible in learning — personalization at scale, real-time skill tracking, content creation in hours. I want to be at that intersection, helping clients realize that potential. Edrevel is doing it at the right moment."

**"Where do you see yourself in 3 years?"**
> "Deepening into product strategy — using patterns I observe across client implementations to help shape Edrevel's roadmap. I see this BA role as building the foundation: deep client understanding + deep product knowledge = product leadership."
